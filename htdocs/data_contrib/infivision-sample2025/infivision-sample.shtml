Contributed EIT Data:

Authors: Lana Chen, Andy Adler
Date: 2025
Brief Description: The dataset represents a sample of healthy human breathing, collected by Infivision Medical Imaging Technology Co. for system evaluation. It includes a series of tidal breaths as well as expiratory and inspiratory breath-holding phases. The data are provided as a .mes file exported from the Infivision EIT System Analysis (ETSA) software.
License: Creative Commons Artistic License
Attribution Requirement: Data are provided as-is, with no formal attribution required. However, acknowledgment of this tutorial is appreciated. The subject has explicitly consented to the public release of these data under these conditions.
Format: EIT data were acquired using an Infivision ET1000 device at a sampling rate of 20 frames/s. The excitation frequency was 20 kHz, current 0.7 mA.
Methods: An Infivision electrode belt was attached to the subject in a supine position and connected to the EIT system. Data were collected during various breathing maneuvers by the subject.
Data: Data (EIT "mes" format)
